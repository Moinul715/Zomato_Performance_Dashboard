Case Study
Challenge:
Zomato noticed a significant decline in orders and revenue in the year 2020 compared to previous years. The objective was to identify the underlying causes and develop strategies to mitigate this decline.

Analysis:
Using the Power BI dashboard, I performed a detailed analysis of the sales data. I filtered the data by city, food category, and month to identify patterns and trends. The line chart showing yearly sales trends indicated a sharp decline in 2020.

Findings:
City-Wise Analysis: Certain cities like Tirupati and Electronic City, Bangalore, had a lesser decline compared to others.

Food Category Analysis: The decline was more pronounced in the Non-Veg category.

Monthly Trends: The decline was most significant during the months of March to June, which coincided with the COVID-19 pandemic lockdowns.

Recommendations:
Promotional Campaigns: Launch targeted promotional campaigns in cities with the highest declines.

Diversify Offerings: Introduce new vegetarian and vegan dishes to attract a wider audience.

Safety Measures: Communicate enhanced safety measures to customers to build confidence during the pandemic.

Solution
Problem:
The significant drop in revenue and orders in 2020 posed a threat to Zomato's business sustainability.

Solution:
Data-Driven Decisions: Using the insights from the dashboard, Zomato can make informed decisions on where to focus their efforts.

Targeted Marketing: By identifying the cities and food categories with the highest decline, targeted marketing campaigns can be developed to address these areas.

Customer Confidence: Emphasize safety and hygiene measures to reassure customers and encourage them to place orders.

Conclusion
This dashboard serves as a powerful tool for analyzing Zomato's food delivery service performance. It provides valuable insights into overall sales, food category preferences, city-wise sales figures, and yearly trends. With interactive filters and visualizations, users can drill down into specific data points to gain a deeper understanding of the business dynamics. Through detailed analysis and problem-solving, Zomato can implement effective strategies to overcome challenges and drive growth.
